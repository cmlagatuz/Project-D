## 1. Warrior of Light — *The Crystal's Vanguard*
**World:** FF I · **First released:** 18 Dec 1987 · **Role:** Vanguard / Fighter · **Difficulty:** 2/5 · **Starter**

One of the four Warriors of Light who bore the crystals in the world's oldest recorded conflict; in the arena, the steadfast champion of Cosmos, embodying order and duty. A frontline anchor who wants to be first in and last out, soaking pressure and peeling for carries, turning every block into an empowered counter.

- **Passive — Crystal Resolve:** blocking/controlling builds Resolve; the next Shield of Light is empowered and heals briefly.
- **Q — Radiant Cut:** short sword-arc cone; outer edge deals bonus damage and briefly slows.
- **W — Shield of Light:** directional guard that greatly reduces frontal damage, then releases a light wave.
- **E — Vanguard Step:** dash to an ally or enemy; arriving on an enemy briefly displaces them.
- **R — Shining Bulwark:** protective field granting allies resistances; marks nearby enemies for focused defense.

**Strengths:** peel, durability, low skill floor. **Weaknesses:** little hard CC, modest kill pressure, kiteable.
**Combos & tips:** aim Shield of Light at the enemy's biggest damage source; Vanguard Step onto an ally to reposition or onto an enemy to knock them off your carry; pop Shining Bulwark before diving.

## 2. Garland — *Knight of the Endless Cycle*
**World:** FF I · **First released:** 18 Dec 1987 · **Role:** Fighter / Vanguard · **Difficulty:** 3/5

The fallen knight bound to a time loop, a servant of Chaos who revels in perpetual battle. A grinding bruiser who ramps armor and power the longer he keeps swinging, so he wants drawn-out fights.

- **Passive — Cycle of Strife:** ability hits grant stacking armor + power (capped); fades out of combat.
- **Q — Greatblade Reap:** broad cone sweep that pulls edge targets slightly inward.
- **W — Iron Advance:** unstoppable wind-up into a short charge that knocks back.
- **E — Chain of Ruin:** chain-blade line that slows and enables a recast lunge.
- **R — Chaosbringer:** empowers size/reach and releases repeated shockwaves for a duration.

**Strengths:** sustained damage + durability, multiple soft controls, disruptive dive. **Weaknesses:** weak burst, resets out of combat, kited by mobility.
**Combos & tips:** Chain of Ruin (slow) → recast lunge → Greatblade Reap to clump; Iron Advance is unstoppable during wind-up — use it to punch through CC; save Chaosbringer for a 5-man.

## 3. Firion — *Wielder of the Wild Rose*
**World:** FF II · **First released:** 17 Dec 1988 · **Role:** Marksman / Fighter · **Difficulty:** 3/5 · **Starter**

A rebel of Fynn who masters every weapon he touches; the wild rose is his symbol of hope. A flexible ranged carry rewarded for weaving weapon types.

- **Passive — Arsenal Rhythm:** using a different ability class than the last grants a stacking adaptive bonus.
- **Q — Spearline:** piercing thrust with a max-range sweet spot.
- **W — Bow Volley:** targeted area volley that reveals struck enemies.
- **E — Blood Blade:** close strike that heals based on damage dealt.
- **R — Wild Rose Arsenal:** rapid weapon projections ending in a controllable finishing shot.

**Strengths:** adaptable range, mixed poke + sustain, flexible builds. **Weaknesses:** lower peak DPS than a pure crit ADC, combo-dependent, squishy in melee.
**Combos & tips:** Spearline → Bow Volley → Blood Blade keeps Arsenal Rhythm stacked and heals off the finisher; aim the ultimate's final shot at a low-health target.

## 4. The Emperor — *Sovereign of Palamecia*
**World:** FF II · **First released:** 17 Dec 1988 · **Role:** Mage · **Difficulty:** 4/5

The ruthless ruler who conquered through sorcery, ambitious enough to seize Heaven and Hell; a schemer of Chaos who never dirties his own hands. A zone-control mage who plays chess with traps.

- **Passive — Dominion:** enemies hit by two separate traps become Exposed (bonus magic damage).
- **Q — Thunder Sigil:** delayed lightning seal on the ground.
- **W — Hell Snare:** hidden zone that arms then roots; visible only up close.
- **E — Imperial Repulse:** cone blast that pushes enemies toward prepared zones.
- **R — Pandemonium:** domain where traps arm faster and bolts strike Exposed targets.

**Strengths:** area denial, objective/chokepoint control, burst on trapped targets. **Weaknesses:** weak without setup, low mobility, punished by hard engage.
**Combos & tips:** pre-place Hell Snare in brush; trigger one trap then a second for Exposed; Imperial Repulse pushes divers back onto your Thunder Sigil.

## 5. Onion Knight — *Prodigy of the Forbidden Jobs*
**World:** FF III · **First released:** 27 Apr 1990 · **Role:** Assassin / Mage · **Difficulty:** 5/5

A gifted youth chosen by the crystals whose strength blooms when underestimated. A high-skill hybrid built around stance dancing between Ninja and Sage.

- **Passive — Job Mastery:** alternating physical/magical casts empowers the next opposite type.
- **Q — Swift Blade:** dash slash; partial cooldown reset on a killing blow / marked target.
- **W — Dualcast:** launches two compact magic bolts.
- **E — Job Shift:** toggle Ninja (mobility) or Sage (range), modifying Q and W.
- **R — Mastered Jobs:** briefly activates both stances and grants a finishing recast.

**Strengths:** very high ceiling, flexible engage/poke, adapts to threats. **Weaknesses:** punishing floor, very squishy, combo-dependent.
**Combos & tips:** Ninja Swift Blade resets through minions; swap to Sage for an empowered Dualcast, then back to escape; alternate physical → magical → physical for Job Mastery.

## 6. Cloud of Darkness — *Herald of the Void*
**World:** FF III · **First released:** 27 Apr 1990 · **Role:** Mage · **Difficulty:** 4/5

The embodiment of the Void, neither cruel nor kind — simply the end of all things. A long-range control mage who suffocates a lane and detonates it.

- **Passive — Particle Beam:** sustained attacks apply Void, increasing damage to that target.
- **Q — Zero Ray:** narrow beam; range extends after a brief charge.
- **W — Dark Flood:** advancing field that slows.
- **E — Unbound Form:** brief untargetable drift (cannot cast during it).
- **R — World of Nothing:** expanding void zone that reduces vision and detonates stored Void.

**Strengths:** range + zoning, scaling, teamfight detonation. **Weaknesses:** immobile, narrow skillshots, low early burst.
**Combos & tips:** land Zero Ray at max range to stack Void safely; Dark Flood a chokepoint; save Unbound Form for the enemy engage; open fights with World of Nothing on the cluster.

## 7. Cecil Harvey — *The Dark Knight Redeemed*
**World:** FF IV · **First released:** 19 Jul 1991 · **Role:** Vanguard / Fighter · **Difficulty:** 4/5 · **Starter**

A Dark Knight who climbed Mount Ordeals to become a Paladin; both selves live within him. A dual-stance frontliner who flexes between aggression and protection.

- **Passive — Dual Nature:** Dark Knight damage fills a Light gauge; Paladin abilities spend it efficiently.
- **Q — Darkness / Holy Edge:** health-cost cleave (Dark) or restorative light slash (Paladin).
- **W — Shadow March / Cover:** pressure aura (Dark) or leap to shield an ally (Paladin).
- **E — Nightfall / Radiant Chain:** slow field (Dark) or protective tether (Paladin).
- **R — Redemption:** swaps stance instantly, cleanses, and empowers the new stance.

**Strengths:** flexible, self-sustaining, flexes bruiser↔protector. **Weaknesses:** jack-of-all-trades damage, self-damage in Dark, stance mismanagement.
**Combos & tips:** farm/poke in Dark to fill the gauge, Redemption into Paladin before a fight to spend it on shields; time Cover for the enemy's burst.

## 8. Kain Highwind — *Dragoon of the Highwind*
**World:** FF IV · **First released:** 19 Jul 1991 · **Role:** Assassin / Fighter · **Difficulty:** 4/5 · **Starter**

Captain of the Baron Dragoons and Cecil's rival, whose spear reaches the heavens. A dive assassin defined by his aerial ultimate.

- **Passive — Dragoon's Poise:** moving toward an isolated enemy grants speed.
- **Q — Lancet Thrust:** linear stab that heals when it hits a champion.
- **W — Vault:** short hop over terrain with a landing strike.
- **E — Dragon Pin:** spear toss that marks and slows.
- **R — High Jump:** leaps off-screen (untargetable), selects a landing area, then crashes down and knocks up.

**Strengths:** elite pick potential, burst + lockdown, dodges with High Jump. **Weaknesses:** all-in/telegraphed, exposed on a whiff, weak sustained damage.
**Combos & tips:** land Dragon Pin first so the slow guarantees High Jump; use the airborne window to dodge a key ability; pair with a follow-up stun.

## 9. Bartz Klauser — *The Wandering Mimic*
**World:** FF V · **First released:** 6 Dec 1992 · **Role:** Fighter · **Difficulty:** 5/5

An easygoing wanderer who mastered countless jobs, mimicking every hero he meets. The ultimate expression-of-mastery fighter.

- **Passive — Freelancer:** small bonuses from each distinct item category owned.
- **Q — Spellblade Arc:** choose a temporary element that changes the secondary effect (slow/burn/heal).
- **W — Ranger Step:** mobile ranged strike usable while repositioning.
- **E — Guard Job:** brief stance converting incoming damage into movement speed.
- **R — Job Parade:** cycles three empowered job actions from a pre-match loadout.

**Strengths:** extreme flexibility, build-adaptive, high ceiling. **Weaknesses:** no dominant strength, loadout committed pre-match, average stats.
**Combos & tips:** pick a Job Parade loadout that covers your team's gaps; match Spellblade Arc's element to the enemy; activate Guard Job as you commit, then reposition.

## 10. Exdeath — *Tyrant of the Great Void*
**World:** FF V · **First released:** 6 Dec 1992 · **Role:** Mage / Vanguard · **Difficulty:** 3/5

An ancient tree that absorbed centuries of sealed evil into armored form, seeking to consume all existence. A tanky control mage who trades mobility for raw presence.

- **Passive — Rooted in the Void:** standing still boosts defenses and stores energy released on the next move/cast.
- **Q — Black Branch:** line of dark roots that erupts after a delay.
- **W — Gravity Well:** pulls units toward its center.
- **E — Void Gate:** paired short portals for projectiles and one champion passage.
- **R — Grand Cross:** large patterned zone with sequential, avoidable detonations and random debuffs.

**Strengths:** durable for a mage, strong pull + zone control, punishes clumps. **Weaknesses:** very low mobility, kited by poke, long ult wind-up.
**Combos & tips:** bank Rooted defenses, then move to unleash stored energy; Gravity Well → Black Branch drags enemies onto the roots; trap them inside Grand Cross with the pull.
