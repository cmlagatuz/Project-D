# Project D — P5 Backlog (Core Gameplay Depth)

> Codename Zantetsuken · CONFIDENTIAL · All original design.
P5 = the deep in-match mechanics beneath the UI/meta of P0-P4: the authoritative
gameplay rules that make the MOBA actually play. Engine-agnostic + unit-tested cores;
server-authoritative simulation.

## 10 systems
1.  Draft/Pick-Ban engine — ordered ban/pick phases, uniqueness, per-team turns.
2.  Objective bounties & buffs — summon/divine/Odin blessings + team buff timers.
3.  Vision & wards — ward placement caps, reveal, expiry, sweeper counters.
4.  Jungle economy — camp values, clear timing, smite, scaling by match time.
5.  Minion waves & lane state — wave composition, push/freeze, cannon cadence.
6.  Turret plating & aggro — plating gold windows, turret aggro/last-hit rules.
7.  Damage & mitigation — armor/MR reduction, penetration, true damage, crits.
8.  Comeback mechanics — shutdown bounties, catch-up XP/gold when behind.
9.  Surrender vote — timed vote, thresholds, remake conditions.
10. Emote/mastery expression — in-match emote wheel + cooldown + spam guard.
